What the review can and cannot say

A compact interpretation guardrail for readers.

SupportedTumor response was preserved/comparable in all 8 classifiable in-vivo breast/mammary comparisons.

This is a direction-of-effect synthesis, not a formal equivalence meta-analysis.

SupportedThoracic skin was directionally consistent in the directly eligible anatomical subset.

Four classifiable reports favored FLASH; non-thoracic skin experiments were not counted in this denominator.

HeterogeneousLung and thoracic immune/hematologic evidence were not uniformly favorable.

Endpoint, timing, model, modality, dose, fractionation, and comparator conditions differ materially across studies.

Do not overgeneralizeNo single dose-rate number defines the biological FLASH effect across this review.

Comparator rate is retained as an interpretive stratum rather than used as an eligibility cutoff.

How to read the atlas

Methods & Denominators

This page mirrors the final review's eligibility and evidence-handling rules rather than inventing a new evidence framework.

Review flow

Core scope

Publication status
English-language, peer-reviewed primary research published January 1, 2014 through August 25, 2026.
FLASH/UHDR boundary
Primary-study-defined FLASH/UHDR with prespecified physical boundary of instantaneous dose rate ≥40 Gy/s or average dose rate ≥10 Gy/s.
Breast/mammary evidence
In vitro, 3D/ex vivo, and in vivo breast or mammary tumor models with eligible FLASH/UHDR exposure.
Thoracic normal tissue
Lung, heart/cardiac tissue, thoracic/hemithoracic/breast/chest-wall skin, and thoracic immune/hematologic outcomes relevant to breast radiotherapy.

Comparator handling

Author-described conventional, standard, conventional-dose-rate, or clinically standard irradiation was retained regardless of numerical rate. Comparator dose rate was not an eligibility threshold; direct biological comparisons were stratified as ≤0.2 Gy/s, >0.2 Gy/s, mixed, or not numerically resolved.

Outcome independence

Linked reports could be retained for distinct eligible outcomes, time points, or newly generated experiments. Reused animals, tissues, images, or public datasets were not counted as new independent evidence for the same outcome.

Skin denominator note

The formal skin synthesis is intentionally restricted to thoracic, hemithoracic, breast, chest-wall, or overlying thoracic skin. Hindlimb, foot, flank, dorsal, ear, and other non-thoracic skin studies can inform broader radiobiology but do not enter the 4-report thoracic-skin denominator.

Data provenance and version
