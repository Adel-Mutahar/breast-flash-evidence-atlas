# Breast FLASH Evidence Atlas

Interactive companion to **FLASH Radiotherapy in Breast Cancer and Breast-Relevant Organs: A Scoping Review of Preclinical Evidence and the Clinical Roadmap**.

**Version:** 1.0  
**Released:** October 12, 2026  
**Formal search end date:** August 25, 2026  
**Evidence base:** 58 eligible reports

## What this site does

- Searches and filters the complete 58-report evidence ledger.
- Separates evidence depth from direction-of-effect denominators.
- Provides domain, modality, comparator, design, direction, and year filters.
- Provides a Citation Hub with DOI links and copyable full references.
- Makes the complete CSV and final **Supplementary Material E2** workbook downloadable.
- Preserves the review's final thoracic-skin, overlap, and comparator handling rules.

## Data provenance

`data.js`, the downloadable CSV, and the website views were regenerated from the attached final **Supplementary Material E2** used for the review. The submitted/published manuscript and Supplementary Material E2 are authoritative if a discrepancy is found.

The formal website summaries match the final manuscript: 58 eligible reports; 35 lung, 18 breast/mammary, 7 thoracic immune/hematologic, 4 thoracic skin, 3 cardiac, and 4 breast-specific planning/dosimetry reports; modalities 28 electron, 13 proton, 12 photon/X-ray, and 5 other/unresolved; direct comparator strata 25 at ≤0.2 Gy/s, 18 at >0.2 Gy/s, 1 mixed, and 1 not numerically resolved.

## Privacy

The site contains no analytics, cookies, account login, or user-tracking code. Standard web-server/GitHub Pages operational logs may still be generated by the hosting provider.

## Deploy to GitHub Pages

1. Create a public repository named `breast-flash-evidence-atlas`.
2. Upload the contents of this folder to the repository root.
3. In GitHub: **Settings → Pages → Build and deployment → Deploy from a branch**.
4. Select branch **main** and folder **/(root)**, then save.
5. The public URL will be `https://<YOUR-GITHUB-USERNAME>.github.io/breast-flash-evidence-atlas/`.
6. Replace `[PUBLIC_URL]` in `MANUSCRIPT_LINK_TEXT.md` with the exact live URL after deployment.

Because all asset paths are relative and `.nojekyll` is included, no build step is required.

## Recommended preservation step after deployment

Create a GitHub release (`v1.0`) containing the final dataset and, after manuscript acceptance or when you are ready to freeze the public record, optionally archive that release in Zenodo for a DOI.

## Version 1.0 final interface

The final Version 1.0 combines the verified 58-report manuscript-aligned evidence ledger with a graph-rich Research Dashboard. The dashboard adds publication activity, modality composition, comparator strata, the breast-tumor evidence pathway, normal-tissue direction, and a clickable thoracic-organ explorer. The Study Explorer, Evidence Map, Citation Hub, Methods & Denominators, downloadable CSV, and Supplementary Material E2 remain the authoritative navigation and data layers.
